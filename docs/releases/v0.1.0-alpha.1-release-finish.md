## Recovery STOP classification

The latest complete guide attempts for both ordinary RC.4 profiles ended with:

```text
RuntimeError: STOP: unclassified unobservable process: {"pid": 3779, "starttime": "3347"}
```

The recorded traceback is a `PermissionError` while inspecting `/proc/3779/fd/0`, handled by `quiescence()` in the exact offline recipe. `transfer()` calls that guard before binary validation, output-directory creation, source SQLite opening and `src.backup()`. A refused inventory is therefore not a database-corruption result or an observed SQLite backup failure.

The condition is deliberately conservative: a same-UID process with unreadable descriptors cannot safely be assumed unrelated. Its name or a successful integrity check cannot establish writer exclusion. The guide's demo supplies an empty `unobservable` list. The historical qualification harness supplies a per-invocation operator assertion for fresh, exclusively owned fixtures and identity-bound ledger entries; these assertions do not prove descriptor absence. Their different outcomes do not establish a regression.

**Conclusion:** this STOP is an execution-environment/process-inventory limitation enforced by an intentional fail-closed safety guard. No database/recovery defect was demonstrated.
